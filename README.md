# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** built using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer purchasing patterns, product categories, and payment methods.

This project transforms raw e-commerce sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and KPI cards, supporting data-driven decision-making.

## 📌 Project Overview

The E-Commerce Sales Analysis project focuses on understanding business performance by analyzing sales revenue, profit, quantity sold, customer contributions, and product sub-categories.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics and enables users to explore performance across quarters and other available filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales revenue and profitability.
- Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Identify states contributing the most to sales.
- Compare product category performance by quantity sold.
- Analyze monthly profit trends and identify loss-making months.
- Understand sales distribution across payment methods.
- Identify customers contributing significantly to revenue.
- Compare profitability across product sub-categories.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Performance by State
A horizontal bar chart compares sales amounts across states, including Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and Delhi.

**Business purpose:** Identify high-performing geographic markets and opportunities to improve sales in lower-performing regions.

### 2. Quantity Sold by Category
A doughnut chart displays the distribution of quantities sold across product categories.

The visible chart indicates approximately:
- Category 1: 63%
- Category 2: 21%
- Category 3: 17%

**Business purpose:** Understand product demand and support inventory planning.


### 3. Monthly Profit Analysis
A column chart displays monthly profit and loss across the year.

**Business purpose:**
- Identify profitable and loss-making months.
- Recognize fluctuations in profitability.
- Investigate seasonal trends and potential causes of losses.

The dashboard shows positive profit in several months and negative profit in others, making monthly profitability an important area for further analysis.

### 4. Sales by Customer
A bar chart compares sales amounts across customers, including Harivansh, Madhav, Madan Mohan, and Shiva.

**Business purpose:** Identify customers contributing significantly to sales and support customer relationship management.

### 5. Quantity Sold by Payment Mode
A doughnut chart visualizes the distribution of quantities sold across payment methods.

**Business purpose:** Understand customer payment preferences and evaluate the adoption of different payment options.

### 6. Profit by Product Sub-Category
A horizontal bar chart compares profit across product sub-categories, including:
- Printers
- Bookcases
- Sarees
- Accessories
- Tables

Printers and bookcases appear among the leading sub-categories by profit in the displayed view.

**Business purpose:** Identify profitable product segments and support product portfolio decisions.

## 🔍 Business Insights

Based on the visible dashboard:

1. **Overall performance:** The dashboard reports sales of 438K, profit of 37K, and a total quantity sold of 6K.
2. **Geographic performance:** Maharashtra has the highest displayed sales among the states shown.
3. **Category concentration:** One category contributes approximately 63% of the displayed quantity, indicating a concentration of demand.
4. **Monthly profitability:** Some months record negative profit, suggesting opportunities to investigate discounts, costs, returns, or product mix.
5. **Product profitability:** Printers and bookcases lead the displayed sub-category profit comparison.
6. **Customer contribution:** The customer-wise chart shows differences in sales contribution, which can help identify high-value customers.

These observations describe the displayed dashboard and should be revalidated against the underlying dataset before being used for business decisions.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could be investigated:

- Study high-performing states to identify practices that could be applied to other regions.
- Maintain appropriate inventory for high-demand categories.
- Investigate loss-making months by examining discount levels, costs, returns, and product mix.
- Evaluate the profitability of individual sub-categories before making pricing or promotional decisions.
- Develop customer retention strategies for high-value customers.
- Monitor payment-mode trends to improve the purchasing experience.
